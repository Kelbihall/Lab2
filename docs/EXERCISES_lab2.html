We calculate the mean and standard deviation of DBH for all trees recorded in 2007 using summarise(). The mean DBH represents the average tree diameter, while the standard deviation indicates how much individual tree diameters vary from the average. A higher standard deviation suggests more variation in tree sizes, whereas a lower standard deviation means most trees have similar diameters.
